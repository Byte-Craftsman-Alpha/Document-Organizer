A LITTLE MORE PEACE OF MIND

Your device.
Your Google Drive.

This is the live, backend-free app build. The public Google client ID is configured. Google Cloud must also allow this website’s origin and your test account.

Once the Cloud settings are confirmed, sign in to your own private vault. Uploaded files and document metadata go directly to your Google Drive.

Flutter in a phone-sized frame. No Vaulty backend. Each account has its own data. Browser cache is session-only. Android adds encrypted offline storage and incoming share imports. Exported copies cannot be recalled.
